# Screenshots

Eight images (and a GIF, further down) of the application running in English with the demo data (`seed-demo`, see the [README](../../README.md#run-locally)). They serve as a presentation of the project; they are not generated by CI and may become outdated if the interface changes. The site is also available in Portuguese (pt-PT, the default); these images and the GIF use English, and on-screen texts are quoted as they appear in `frontend/messages/en.json`. Names of clubs, pitches and people come from the seed and stay as they are (for example `Futebol 7` and `Ténis 1`).

## Data

- **Base:** `seed-demo` ran on 2026-11-11, on a fresh database, for the screenshots and the GIF (redone in English that day). It creates 5 owners, 60 players, 10 clubs, 43 pitches, 5 blocks and about 10,700 bookings, which cover the previous 60 days and the next 14. All addresses are `example.test` and the password of the demo accounts is the one in the README.
- **Periods used:** the statistics (`screenshot-owner-statistics-club`, `screenshot-admin-dashboard` and `screenshot-admin-top-occupation-registrations`) use 15/09/2026 to 14/10/2026, a range entirely inside the data. The other four screenshots use one day, chosen by the script: the first weekday (the price band covers Monday to Friday) in the next 14 days on which Futsal A is free at 21:00 for three weeks in a row and Lisboa Padel Club can take the block and the customer booking. On 2026-11-11 that was Friday 13 November.
- **Added by the capture script after the seed**, in English, through the API as demo owners and through the interface as a demo player, to show features the seed does not create: a price band on Futsal A of Arena Futsal Boavista (Monday to Friday, 18:00–00:00, €46.00); the weekly series of `jogador01@example.test` (Fridays 13, 20 and 27 November at 21:00), created with `Confirm 3 bookings` right after the `screenshot-booking-summary-weekly-repeat` capture (so `screenshot-my-bookings-upcoming-series` shows it and `screenshot-booking-summary-weekly-repeat` still shows the three dates free); a block on Padel 1 of Lisboa Padel Club (09:00–12:00, reason `Floodlight maintenance`); and a booking for the demo customer "Rui Teixeira" on Padel 2 (15:00–16:30). The seed's own free-text values (block reasons, cancellation reasons) are in Portuguese; none of them is on screen.

## How they were made

The script [`docs/demo/capture-screenshots.mjs`](../demo/capture-screenshots.mjs) (`npm run screenshots` in `docs/demo`; steps in the [demo README](../demo/README.md)) against the full stack with `docker compose` in a separate project and `seed-demo`: Chromium with Playwright, 1440 px wide window, `Europe/Lisbon` time zone, English (the `NEXT_LOCALE=en` cookie and the `en-GB` locale), light theme (the application has no dark theme), production build (no development overlays). Each PNG is reduced to a palette of 128 colours by `ffmpeg` and was checked by eye: only demo data appears, with no secrets, tokens or browser frame.
